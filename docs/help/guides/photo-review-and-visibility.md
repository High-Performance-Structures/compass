## Upload and Find Photos {#upload-find}

Open project **Photos**, choose files, confirm the date, type, caption, and phase, then upload. Originals belong in the mapped Drive location while Compass stores project relationships and display metadata. New uploads begin internal and awaiting review.

Use date, phase, type, review, visibility, and sort controls to narrow the library. Filters never change permission by themselves.

Selection photos (finish and fixture choices) are in **Selections**, not Photos. They never appear in the Photos library, its counts, the owner and trade-partner Photos pages, client updates, dashboards, or daily logs, and the photo types here leave out Selection.

## Review the Full Image {#review-image}

Open the full image before approving it. Confirm the project, date, phase, type, orientation, clarity, and background details. A suggested phase is not approval.

Look for faces, addresses, license plates, receipts, safety incidents, disputes, defects, internal notes, or other information that should remain limited.

## Approve and Set Visibility {#approve-visibility}

Select the intended photos and apply a type and each permitted audience. If no external audience is selected, the photo remains internal. Use **Reject** when it should not enter the approved project library.

- **Owner** exposes it to assigned owners according to project access.
- **Subs/vendors** exposes it to assigned project partners as permitted.
- **Public** approves it for a broader sharing workflow.

These choices are independent. Owner-visible is not public, and public approval does not make the underlying Drive folder public.

## Logs and Owner Updates {#logs-owner-updates}

Attaching a photo to a daily log does not make it owner-visible. An owner update should include a photo only after approval and owner visibility are confirmed.

## Batch Actions {#batch-actions}

Use filtered selection only after reviewing the active filters. Confirm every selected image before applying visibility. Compass clears selection after an action so an earlier batch is not accidentally reused.

**Delete** removes the selected photos after a confirmation that shows the count. Hidden duplicate copies go with them, the files move to the Google Drive trash, and Activity keeps a full record of the delete. Photos shown in a client update or social post are kept until they are taken out of it. Projects whose records are on hold, such as a legal hold, can't delete photos.

## Prepare Photos for Social Publishing {#social-publishing}

Public-shareable approval makes a reviewed photo eligible for the project's **Social posts** workflow; it does not publish it. Confirm the approved public project title and town/city, department destinations, text, and image contents before a public post. See [Social posts](/dashboard/help/social-posts) for setup, drafting, publishing, and failure recovery.
