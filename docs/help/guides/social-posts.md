## Complete Project Social Setup {#setup}

Open the project's **Social posts** page. Use **Complete project social setup** or **Edit public project identity** to confirm the department, approved public project title, and town/city. Public identity should protect the client's privacy; do not substitute a private client name or street address. The dashboard reminder prefers eligible active projects and can point to setup when identity is incomplete; historical and closed jobs are excluded from that reminder.

## Verify Department Destinations {#destinations}

An administrator connects Facebook, Instagram, or X destinations in **Settings → Integrations** for the correct department. Confirm the actual destination before drafting. “Connected” does not prove that a platform will accept a post: Settings and the social workspace can show the last publishing error. Missing department setup, missing accounts, and provider capability failures need their specific correction.

## Prepare the Draft and Photos {#draft}

Choose the intended destinations and approved public-shareable photos. Use the **Photo review** link to review and approve photos before using them. Draft the heading, text, and tags, or review an AI suggestion when available. Inspect images for faces, private details, addresses, and sensitive project conditions. Owner approval and public approval are separate.

Facebook can reuse an album with the same privacy-safe public title or create one when absent. Instagram requires at least one photo and currently requires JPEG photos. Save the draft, reopen it, and check the destination-specific content before publishing.

## Publish and Review the Outcome {#publish}

Authorized publishing staff use **Publish now** and confirm that identity, photos, heading, text, tags, and every destination are approved. Public publishing is an external action and may not be reversible from Compass. Read the outcome for each destination: a partial or failed result is not full success. Inspect the stored failure before retrying so a successful destination is not mistaken for an unsent post.

Drafts can be edited and deleted with the available permission-appropriate actions and confirmation. Deleting a draft does not remove a post already published on a social platform. Use the platform and the approved correction process for a public mistake.
