## Start an Update {#start-update}

The preferred workflow starts in **Daily Logs**: filter to the reporting period, select only appropriate logs, and choose **Draft owner update**. You can also open **Owner Updates** from the project to review existing drafts and published updates.

## Review the Draft {#review-draft}

Check the title and period, project and owner information, summary, work completed, next steps, schedule language, selected photos, captions, and links. Remove duplicate or internal metadata and distinguish confirmed commitments from tentative plans.

## Photo and Audience Safety {#photo-safety}

Use only photos approved for owner visibility. Open them at full size and inspect receipts, personal information, issue documentation, and background details before including them. Owner visibility does not mean public visibility.

## Publish and Share {#publish-share}

Publishing changes a working draft into an approved owner-facing record. Confirm required project-manager review first. When you publish, the project's owner portal users get a notification in Compass, on their phone, and by email, unless they turned off **Owner updates** in their own settings. The publish (and any recall) is recorded in the **Activity** log. Sharing controls may provide a print/save-PDF view, Compass link, or prepared email. Review the actual output and recipient access before sending.

Use the deployed Compass address, not a local or preview URL. A Compass link cannot override a restricted Drive file's sharing settings.

## Correct a Published Update {#correct-published}

Do not quietly overwrite a material client communication. Notify the project manager or administrator and preserve a clear correction trail.

## Draft or Redraft with Jarvis {#draft-with-jarvis}

Use the update editor's Jarvis drafting action to turn the selected project context into a proposed owner narrative. Compass shows drafting progress and prevents another overlapping draft request. Wait for completion, then review the generated text, reporting period, source logs, schedule claims, captions, and links. Drafting uses the organization's configured AI service; if it is unavailable, retain your manual text and report the error. An AI draft is not publication or owner approval.

## Delete an Unneeded Draft {#delete-draft}

Use **Delete draft** only when the draft should be removed, then review the confirmation. Deletion cannot be undone and returns you to the update list. Published updates require the appropriate correction process; this action is for drafts. Remove a daily log from any draft or published update using it before attempting to delete that log.

## Quick Check {#quick-check}

- [ ] The reporting period and included logs match.
- [ ] Text is concise, client-facing, and final.
- [ ] Every photo is approved for the owner.
- [ ] Links and recipient access were tested.
- [ ] The final view was reviewed before sharing.
