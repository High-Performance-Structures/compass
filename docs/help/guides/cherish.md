## What CHERISH Is {#what-it-is}

CHERISH is a place for employees to notice good work and say so. Each submission is tied to one of seven company values: **Camaraderie**, **Honor**, **Excellence**, **Reliability**, **Integrity**, **Servitude**, and **Humility**. Approved recognition appears as a short story on the dashboard and in the Field App for 24 hours, then stays in each person's archive.

CHERISH is for employees only. Owners, trade partners, vendors, and guests cannot submit, review, or see it. Every submission is held for review first; nothing reaches the team until someone with **CHERISH review** access approves it.

## Ways to Use It {#ways-to-use}

CHERISH works best as a habit rather than an award. Some ways teams use it:

- **Thank someone the day it happens.** A crew member who stayed late to protect a pour, an office teammate who caught a billing error, a superintendent who handled a hard client conversation well.
- **Share a project win.** A passed inspection, a finished milestone, a compliment from a homeowner, or a punch list closed early. Wins remind the whole company what good looks like.
- **Make the values concrete.** New hires learn what "Reliability" or "Humility" means faster from real stories than from a poster. Reviewers can point onboarding conversations at recent examples.
- **Recognize quietly.** Send a shout-out to just one person when public praise would embarrass them, or when the note is personal.
- **Raise a concern early.** A **Private concern** goes only to reviewers. Use it for something leadership should know before it becomes a bigger problem, such as a safety habit slipping, a process that keeps failing, or a team under strain.
- **Start meetings with recognition.** Open a weekly or monthly meeting with the last few approved stories, or have reviewers pull examples from the archive for reviews, recognition programs, or board and company updates.
- **Follow up with a card.** Reviewers can turn approved recognition into a mailed handwritten card, so the thanks reaches the person at home.

## Share a CHERISH {#share}

Open **CHERISH** from the sidebar, or use the CHERISH section in the Field App on a phone. The Field App saves submissions while offline and sends them when the phone reconnects.

1. Choose a **Response type**: **Shoutout**, **Project win**, or **Private concern**.
2. For a shout-out, choose **Who should see this?** Leave it on **Whole company** to share it with everyone, or pick one employee so only they see it.
3. Write the **Message**. Say what happened and who helped.
4. Choose the value it shows. Compass suggests a value from the words in your message; change it if another value fits better.
5. Tick **Submit anonymously** if you don't want your name shown. Your name will not appear in CHERISH review, dashboard stories, or Field App recognition.
6. Select **Submit CHERISH feedback**.

The submission goes to the review queue. Nothing is shared until it is approved.

## Who Sees What {#visibility}

- **Shoutout to the whole company** and **Project win**: after approval, every employee sees the story for 24 hours, then it stays in their archive.
- **Shoutout to one person**: after approval, only that employee sees it.
- **Private concern**: only staff granted **CHERISH review** access can read it. It is never shown as a story.

## Stories, Love This, and Private Replies {#stories}

Approved stories play at the top of the dashboard and in the Field App for 24 hours. Use **Replay today's CHERISH stories** to watch them again. Select **Love this** to react; the count shows how many people loved it. To answer the person who shared it, type in **Reply privately** and send. The reply opens a direct message with them. Anonymous stories can't be replied to, so the author stays anonymous.

After 24 hours a story leaves the dashboard. Company stories and shout-outs shared with you stay under **Your archive** on the CHERISH page.

## Review Submissions {#review}

Staff granted **CHERISH review** access open **CHERISH Review** from the sidebar. Grant this access in **Settings → Permissions** only to people who should read private concerns.

- **Approve** publishes a shout-out or project win to its audience, either the whole company or the one chosen employee.
- **Archive** removes a submission from the queue without publishing it. Archiving an approved company story also removes it from the team stream.
- Search the archive to find past submissions, for example to gather examples for a meeting or review.
- **Mail a physical card** turns approved recognition into a handwritten card. See [Greeting Cards](/dashboard/help/greeting-cards).

Read private concerns carefully, follow up through the right channel, and respect anonymity. Don't try to work out who sent an anonymous submission.

## Quick Check {#quick-check}

- [ ] The message says what happened and who helped.
- [ ] The value fits the story.
- [ ] The audience is right: whole company, one person, or a private concern for reviewers.
- [ ] I ticked **Submit anonymously** if I don't want my name shown.
- [ ] Reviewers approved or archived every waiting submission.
