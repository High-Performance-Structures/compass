## Choose What to Share {#choose-type}

Open **CHERISH** and choose **Shoutout**, **Project win**, or **Private concern**. Use a shoutout to recognize a person, a project win for a team accomplishment, and a private concern for something leadership should review without publishing it to the company stream. Write enough specific context for a reviewer to understand what happened without including information that does not belong with the chosen audience.

## Submit Recognition or a Concern {#submit}

For a shoutout, choose either the whole company or one employee. Enter a message between 3 and 1,200 characters. A project win is intended for company recognition. A private concern stays in the restricted leadership review workflow.

After you write the message, Compass suggests the CHERISH value that best fits: **Camaraderie, Honor, Excellence, Reliability, Integrity, Servitude,** or **Humility**. Review the suggestion and change it when another value is more accurate. The suggestion is guidance, not a final classification.

Choose anonymous submission only when needed. Anonymous removes your name from user-facing review, dashboard, and Field recognition surfaces; it does not change the selected audience or bypass review. Every submission begins in **Needs review**.

## Understand Audience and Review {#review}

Only staff granted CHERISH review access can open **CHERISH review**. A reviewer can approve recognition, archive it, or acknowledge a private concern. Approved company recognition can appear in the team stream. A personal shoutout is visible to its selected recipient. Private concerns remain leadership-only even after they are acknowledged.

Reviewers should confirm the type, audience, value, wording, and anonymity choice before approving. Approval controls publication; it is not a substitute for resolving a concern through the appropriate leadership process.

## Find a Past Story {#archive}

Approved recognition appears as a story for its audience for 24 hours, then remains available in the appropriate CHERISH archive. **Your archive** includes company stories and shoutouts shared directly with you. Use archive search rather than submitting a duplicate when you need to find an earlier story. Reviewers can also archive an active team-stream story when it should no longer be shown.

## Quick Check {#quick-check}

- [ ] I selected shoutout, project win, or private concern intentionally.
- [ ] The audience and named recipient are correct.
- [ ] I reviewed the suggested CHERISH value.
- [ ] I understand whether my name will be shown.
- [ ] I know the submission begins in Needs review.
