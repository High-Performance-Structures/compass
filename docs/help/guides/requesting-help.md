## Protect the Work First {#protect-work}

Stop repeating an action that could duplicate a message, invoice, P.O., RFI, upload, invitation, or Sage entry. Note the project and record number, preserve unsaved text when possible, and keep the page and exact error available.

Never put passwords, API keys, authentication codes, reset tokens, Sage credentials, or private keys into a report or support message.

## Use Contextual Help {#contextual-help}

Use the small Compass Help symbol beside an unfamiliar tool to see its official summary without leaving your work. Select **Read full guide** for the detailed, searchable procedure and related safety notes.

For a product defect, access problem, or missing documentation, submit feedback even if the guide helps you continue. That gives the product team a durable report.

## Submit a Useful Report {#submit-report}

Include the project, page, record, approximate time and timezone, device/browser or app, what you did, what you expected, what happened, and the exact message. State what you already tried and whether you stopped before retrying.

For access requests, include the feature and whether you need to view, create, edit, approve, delete, or administer it. Never borrow another person's account.

## Authentication and Sync {#authentication-sync}

For sign-in or invitation problems, include the email used, platform, time, exact message, and whether the link stayed on the same device/browser. Do not include the secret link or token.

For offline sync, preserve the device data and report the project, item, time, waiting count, and connection state. For Sage, report the Compass record, project, company, amount, status, and whether someone may have entered the same transaction directly.

## Escalate Urgent Issues {#urgent-issues}

Contact an administrator directly when data may be exposed to the wrong audience, a financial record may be duplicated, staff cannot reach active work, offline records appear at risk, or an outage blocks several users.

## Report Missing Help {#missing-help}

If a tooltip or search result does not match the visible product, report the page, control label, and incorrect guidance. Help content is part of the product and should be corrected with workflow changes.

## Quick Check {#quick-check}

- [ ] I stopped before creating duplicates.
- [ ] I included project, page, record, time, and platform.
- [ ] I explained expected and actual behavior.
- [ ] I preserved the exact message without including secrets.
- [ ] I identified access, offline, accounting, or product behavior clearly.
