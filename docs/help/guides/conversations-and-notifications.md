## Choose a Conversation {#choose-conversation}

Use a project channel for communication the project team should find with the job record, a direct message for private coordination, a staff channel for ongoing company discussion, and an announcement for important audience-wide information. Do not leave a project decision only in a private message when the project team needs its record.

## Send a Message {#send-message}

Open the correct channel, confirm its name and project context, write the message, mention only people whose attention is required, attach permitted files, and send. Include searchable context such as the project number, subject, company, or scope.

Mentions may trigger in-app, email, push, or text notifications according to the recipient's preferences and configured services.

## Email People Outside Compass {#project-email}

In a project's **Messages** workspace, staff can choose **New email** to send a project update to people who do not have Compass accounts. Add recipients from active project contacts, search the owner/vendor/internal directory, or enter an email address directly. Use **To**, **Cc**, and **Bcc** according to who should see the address list, then review the recipient counts before sending.

Compass saves a private draft while you compose. Use **Drafts** to reopen unfinished messages, replies, or emails; only the person who created a draft can see it. Leaving the composer waits for the current draft save. **Discard draft** removes it from Drafts and can be undone only before leaving that screen. Do not assume a draft was sent.

The email is saved in the project's message history and appears under **Sent** after it is sent. Replies to its tracked project address return to that conversation. The sender and project staff can see external replies; active Compass recipients can also see and reply in the project conversation. A Bcc recipient has a private Compass view, and their address and replies stay hidden from other external recipients.

Recipients do not gain Compass access merely because they receive email. A reply written inside Compass follows the audience notice shown above the composer: it may be private to project staff or shared with the active Compass recipients of that email. Use **New email** when you need a different external audience. If delivery fails, the original sender can retry the exact saved email. When delivery is uncertain, check the project history and provider outcome before attempting another send.

## Attachments {#attachments}

Use **Upload files** for a new file or **Project files** to browse the project's existing Drive folders. Choosing a project file attaches a copy to the message; the original stays unchanged. Project email allows at most 10 attachments totaling 18 MB. Wait until every file says **Ready** before sending, then open the result from the saved message to verify it.

Attachments saved with a private draft remain private with that draft. A preview in a conversation does not change the original file's project-wide visibility. For external email, Compass sends the attachments as files; project Drive viewer links included by another workflow may have their own access rules.

## Message Actions {#message-actions}

Edit your own message to correct a mistake when allowed. Delete only when the removal is permitted and appropriate for the project record. Reactions are quick acknowledgments, not formal approvals. Archive an inactive channel when its history should remain searchable.

## Announcements {#announcements}

Authorized staff can create an announcement and select its audience. Choose narrowly: staff, owners, project partners, or everyone only when the content truly applies. An organization-wide announcement is not a substitute for a project message.

## Text Project Updates {#text-project-updates}

Send a project update to its department number:

- Open Range / Design: **719-630-8767**
- Nu-Tech: **719-686-0770**
- HPS: **719-900-8850**

Start the text with the project number, followed by a supported tag and the update. Attach photos or video to the same text when needed. For example: `H-430-1900 [DAILY LOG] Crew arrived at 7:00. Framing started.`

For the complete active-project list or basic texting instructions, use the HPS number only: text `[list]` or `[help]` to **719-900-8850**. Project updates may come from any phone number; updates from unrecognized numbers are held for internal review.

## Follow Up on the Staff Message Desk {#message-desk}

Every call or message logged on the **Staff Message Desk** has an owner and a status: **New**, **Follow-up needed**, **In progress**, **Waiting on contact**, or **Closed**. Open a message to add a follow-up note, change its status, or reassign it. Each change is kept in the message's history.

Open messages are flagged as they age, counted in business days. The defaults are:

- **Needs first response**: still New after one business day.
- **Aging**: no follow-up for two business days.
- **Stale**: no follow-up for five business days.

Admins can change these in **Settings → Workflows**.

When a message assigned to you goes **Stale**, three things happen. Your **Staff Message Desk** link in the sidebar turns red with a count, you get an urgent reminder in notifications, and the bell turns red until you read it. Following up on the message clears the reminder. If it later goes stale again, you get one new reminder. Admins can turn these reminders off in Settings.

The towers at the top show how many open messages are in each group. Taller, redder towers mean more messages are waiting, and clicking a tower lists only those messages. To update several messages at once, tick them and use **Set status** or **Reassign to**. Any status change, note, or reassignment counts as follow-up and clears the flag.

## Notification Preferences {#notification-preferences}

In **Settings**, confirm the timezone and choose supported in-app, email, push, and text notifications. SMS requires the user's own current consent. Save changes and use a test action when available; never enable text delivery for another person without consent.

## Use Drafts, Sent, and Global History {#drafts-and-sent}

The project **Messages** workspace has **Inbox**, **Drafts**, **Sent**, **Unread**, **Needs reply**, **Saved**, and **Archived** views. Search subjects and messages to find an existing exchange. **Drafts** contains your unfinished messages, emails, and replies; use **Save draft** and confirm it saved before leaving. Drafts are private to you. Use the available discard action deliberately: a new composition can offer **Undo discard**, while discarding a conversation reply warns that it cannot be recovered.

**Sent** helps you review what you sent. Staff with project access can use **Global** for the project's shared message history. A personal inbox or private email view is not a guarantee that the sent project record is hidden from authorized project staff. Read the audience notice above a reply: it may be private to you and project staff or shared with all active Compass recipients. Use **New email** when a separate external email or a narrower recipient list is required.

## Join and Use Compass Talk {#talk}

Authorized internal staff can open **Talk** and check the private setup screen before choosing **Join meeting**. Use **Preview camera**, **Test microphone**, and the device selectors to verify the right equipment. The preview stays private until joining. Background choices include Off, Blur with adjustable strength, and personal background images. **Stronger background cleanup** hides uncertain edges but can trim fine hair or details; check the preview. Uploaded personal backgrounds can be removed with confirmation.

During a call, use the microphone and camera controls, screen sharing, **Background & Settings**, and picture-in-picture when supported by the browser. **Show Notes & Transcript** opens the side panel; hiding it keeps the current tab, draft notes, and capture state. Save notes with the provided control before leaving. Resizing the window keeps call controls accessible.

Read the call's recovery message when a device fails. Check browser and operating-system permission, choose **System default** or another device, then use **Try again** or **Try alternate microphone method** when offered. If the background cannot start, choose Off or join without video. **Leave** exits only your session; **End for Everyone** is available only when your meeting role permits it. Review the exit confirmation before continuing.

## Use the Notification Inbox {#notification-inbox}

The bell is an inbox for unread and recent items. Open a notification to go to its source, or use its read control. Reading a supported message, RFI, or other linked item also clears its corresponding unread notification, so the bell and the dashboard's **Messages** layer reflect what still needs attention. An urgent unread reminder can turn the bell red. Reading the reminder clears its unread alert; completing the underlying work is a separate action.

## Ask Jarvis About Conversations {#jarvis-messages}

Ask **Jarvis** which conversations have unread messages, to summarize recent messages, or to find a phrase in channels you can see. Its built-in conversation tools read channels and messages; they do not send or edit them. Use the links in the answer to verify context and respond in the appropriate workspace. See [Ask Jarvis](/dashboard/help/ask-jarvis) for the full capability guide.
