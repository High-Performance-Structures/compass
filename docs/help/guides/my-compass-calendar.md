## What My Compass Is {#overview}

**My Compass** is a calendar Compass makes in your own Google Calendar. It shows the Compass work assigned to you, so you can see it next to your meetings on your phone or computer. It sits alongside your other calendars, and you can show, hide, or recolor it in Google Calendar like any other calendar.

Compass is in charge of this calendar. When a date changes in Compass, the calendar updates within a few minutes. Items drop off when they're completed, reassigned, or moved outside the date range. Changes you make to these items in Google are replaced, so make changes in Compass. Each item has an **Open in Compass** link.

## What's on It {#whats-on-it}

Depending on your company's settings, My Compass shows:

- **Schedule items assigned to you**, from each job's schedule
- **To-dos assigned to you**, on their due date
- **Milestones** on jobs where you're on the project team

Items from open and warranty jobs appear from a couple of weeks back to a few months ahead. They show as all-day items marked **free**, so they don't block your availability, and they don't send reminders.

## Turn It On or Off {#turn-on}

Open **Settings → Integrations** and find **My Compass calendar**. Use the switch to turn your calendar on or off. Turning it off removes the calendar from your Google Calendar. **Update now** refreshes it right away, and **Open in Google Calendar** takes you to it.

If you delete the calendar in Google, Compass stops updating it until you turn it back on in Settings.

## When an Item Is Missing {#missing-items}

Assignments in Compass are often stored as names. Compass matches your display name, your first and last name, and your email. If you're sometimes assigned under another name, such as "Wesley Jones" for Wes, add it under **Other names you're assigned under**. Matching items appear within a few minutes.

Schedule items assigned only to the company (for example, "High Performance Structures, Inc.") aren't on anyone's personal calendar. Assign the item to a person in Compass to put it on their calendar.

## Company Settings {#company-settings}

Admins manage My Compass in **Settings → Workflows → My Compass calendar**:

- **Calendars**: Off, **Test people only** (choose who), or **All staff**
- **What goes on each calendar**: assigned schedule items, assigned to-dos, and team milestones
- **Days back** and **Days ahead**: how much of the past and future is shown

Staff with a company Google Workspace account can use My Compass. Each person can still turn their own calendar off.
